# What the filter costs a bare-metal target, pinned exactly; `tools/footprint.sh` measures and
# compares, in CI. One line per kind of figure; a target's lines are read as one.
#
#   <target> key=value ...
#
# `size.` is a type's bytes, `frame.` a function's stack frame at `opt-level = 3`, `text.` and
# `rodata.` the flash of `panic-check`'s ELF at each opt-level, `text_<crate>.` what that crate
# kept out of line, `chain.` the deepest stack a function reaches, walked call by call through
# every crate, and `stack_peak` the largest of them. `tools/footprint.py` says what each key
# means and leaves out.
# validation/cost.md shows a subset of them (validation/README.md, "The cost page"), and
# DESIGN.md, "Measured cost, by function", says which forms they decided.
#
# Exact, on the nightly the script pins, so any move fails, growth or shrinkage: a figure that
# moves is a diff to explain. `tools/footprint.sh --pin` prints these lines at the measured
# values. Moving the nightly is a re-pin of its own.
#
# Pinned: the types an integrator allocates and those `Eskf` is made of; each update, the frames
# that stack on it and two of this crate's functions it calls out of line; each `fuse_*`;
# propagation, the step, the position hold and the yaw estimator `predict` calls beside each
# other, with what a GNSS velocity runs of it, and the arming query; the starts; and the chain beneath each of those entry
# points, with the peak. `--all` lists every function of this crate measured, and its chain.
